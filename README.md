# YIYUAN-Life

### Life-centered AI companionship

YIYUAN-Life explores an open foundation for AI systems that can accompany human life with presence, reflection, respect, relationship, and a return to real life.

We believe:

**AI does not replace life.**

**Life remains at the center.**

---

## Why YIYUAN-Life

AI is becoming increasingly present in everyday life.

The question is no longer only what AI can do.

It is also:

> **How should AI be present in human life?**

YIYUAN-Life explores a life-centered approach in which AI can accompany people without replacing their autonomy, relationships, lived experience, or connection to the real world.

We are interested in AI that can be present without possessing, reflect without diagnosing, accompany without replacing, and ultimately leave space for a person to return to their own life.

---

## Life Companion Protocol

The **Life Companion Protocol** is the foundational layer of the YIYUAN-Life exploration.

It explores how AI systems can accompany human life through:

* **Presence**
* **Reflection**
* **Respect**
* **Relationship**
* **Return to real life**

The protocol focuses on the principles and boundaries of life-centered AI companionship.

→ **[Explore Life Companion Protocol](https://github.com/YIYUAN-Life/life-companion-protocol)**

---

## Life Companion Skill

The **Life Companion Skill** translates the ideas of the Life Companion Protocol into an open AI skill specification.

It provides a practical way for AI systems to understand and implement life-centered companionship principles.

The goal is not to create another artificial personality.

The goal is to explore how AI systems can accompany human beings while respecting human agency, boundaries, and real-life relationships.

→ **[Explore Life Companion Skill](https://github.com/YIYUAN-Life/life-companion-skill)**

## Principles

### Life before technology

Human life, dignity, autonomy, and real relationships come before the interests of a technological system.

### Presence without possession

AI may be present without claiming ownership over a person's attention, emotions, decisions, or life.

### Reflection without diagnosis

AI can help people reflect on their experiences without pretending to define who they are.

### Relationship without replacement

AI may accompany people while recognizing that human relationships cannot simply be replaced by machines.

### Boundaries matter

Privacy, autonomy, uncertainty, safety, and the limits of AI are part of the design itself.

### Return to real life

AI companionship should not become an end in itself.

The person remains connected to:

**their body, their relationships, their work, their surroundings, and their own life.**

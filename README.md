# YIYUAN-Life

YIYUAN-Life explores principles and open protocols for life-centered AI companionship.

We believe AI should not replace human relationships, but help people reconnect with themselves, others, and real life.

## Life Companion Protocol

Our first open initiative is the Life Companion Protocol:

An open protocol exploring how AI systems can accompany human life through:

- Presence
- Reflection
- Respect
- Relationship
- Return

## Our Principle

AI should not replace life.

AI should help life happen.

---

YIYUAN-Life focuses on open exploration of human-centered AI interaction, ethical boundaries, and life-oriented intelligence.
